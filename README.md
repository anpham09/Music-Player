# Music-Player

## Introduction:
Usually all of my favorite songs are not on a same music platform, while the platforms that have all of them are not free. That is why I decided to make my own music player combining my favorite songs^.^ 
Now I add more features to make my fav videos page which I can combine my own fav youtube videos and self uploaded videos, this watch later page lets me put in the videos I am interested in but didnt have chance to watch them

## Features:
- A music player with preload songs
- Different cursors for each feature>v<
- A star slider icon!!
- Full heart when paused and empty heart when playing♡♥
- The disk spins when the song is playing!
- MP3 player design with decorative buttons
- Click the image in the corner to open the playlist and choose a song
- Loop button to repeat the current song until I turn it off
- A random song selected every time I refresh the page
- Two tabs: Songs and Videos
- A Watch Later list for YouTube videos and my own uploaded videos
- Add videos to Favorites or remove them after watching
- A Favorites list with the same corner-image design as the songs playlist
- Upload my own video files!
- Mini screen for watching videos while doing other things
